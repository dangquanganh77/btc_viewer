ACT_01: ORIGIN

THE MANIFESTO

BORN ON A RENTED COURT.

Beyond the Court started with a simple observation by a group of high school students in Da Nang: the youth athletic scene had immense untapped potential, but lacked a unified platform that meant something more than just winning.

In March 2026, we launched "The First Step"—a U21 3v3 basketball tournament. We handled the logistics, the sponsors, the match schedules, and the venue. The rule was simple: bring absolute intensity to the court, and give absolute support to the city.

100% of tournament profits fund local charity.

ACT_02: MISSION

ELEVATE THE GAME.
UPLIFT THE CITY.

We organize high-tier youth athletic events—from 3v3 basketball to open division football—to create a professional ecosystem for Da Nang's youth. The sweat equity generated by these athletes directly translates into financial aid for those who need it most.

ACT_03: PHILOSOPHY

01. RAW COMPETITION

No shortcuts. We engineer tournaments that demand peak performance, professional logistics, and absolute grit from every team that steps on the court.

02. TANGIBLE IMPACT

Every event is a charity engine. Registration fees and sponsor funds are rigorously managed, with 100% of event profits delivered directly to Da Nang's local charities.

03. YOUTH LED

Operated entirely by a dedicated student-led roster. We draft the rules, secure the venues, manage the media, and execute the game-day operations.
